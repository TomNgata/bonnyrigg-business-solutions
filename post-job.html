Client Questions

Frequently Asked Questions for Clients

Everything you need to know about posting jobs and protecting your privacy on BonnyRigg.

Is it really free for the general public to post a job?
Yes. Clients pay KES 0 to post requests, receive quotes, and hire service providers. BonnyRigg is funded solely by verified service providers paying KES 200 from their M-Pesa wallet when they unlock a matched lead.
Who can see my phone number after I submit?
Your phone number is masked on the public marketplace. Only vetted providers who operate in your service category, accept your stated budget, and commit KES 200 to unlock your request can view your contact details.
Am I obligated to hire the first provider who calls me?
Not at all. You remain in complete control to compare qualifications, quotations, and timelines, and choose the provider who best fits your needs.
