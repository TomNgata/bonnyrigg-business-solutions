Who We Are

Building Trust & Commercial Velocity in Kenya’s Service Economy.

BonnyRigg Business Solutions was founded to solve a real everyday friction in Nairobi and across Kenya: connecting ready-to-hire clients with capable, vetted service businesses without wasted time or mismatched pricing.

Our Guiding Ethos

When expectations around scope and budget are transparent from minute one, local SMEs close more work and clients enjoy dependable, high-trust service delivery.

Our Story & Purpose

Global Marketplace Discipline. Local Kenyan Execution.

Across Kenya, skilled advocates, certified mechanics, accountants, plumbers, engineers, and consultants spend far too many hours chasing unverified leads—or quoting clients whose budgets do not match the scope of work. Meanwhile, busy homeowners and companies struggle to find dependable professionals quickly.

Drawing on international service-marketplace best practices and deep Kenyan commercial leadership, BonnyRigg Business Solutions brings structured price-point matching and seamless M-Pesa wallet micro-payments together—making quality customer acquisition accessible to every serious Kenyan service business from KES 600.

Our Commercial Commitment
Our Mission To empower Kenyan SMEs and independent professionals with predictable, budget-aligned client leads while giving the public a fast, spam-free way to hire trusted experts.
Our Vision To be East Africa’s most trusted two-sided lead generation marketplace—known for pricing transparency, M-Pesa simplicity, and measurable business growth.
