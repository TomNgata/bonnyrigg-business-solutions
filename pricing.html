Wallet & Billing FAQ

Common Questions About M-Pesa Wallets

Straightforward answers on deposits, lead deductions, and wallet credits.

Why is the minimum starting deposit KES 600?
A KES 600 minimum deposit activates your provider profile and immediately credits enough balance for 3 full lead unlocks (3 × KES 200). There is no separate sign-up or registration fee—every shilling of your KES 600 goes directly toward unlocking leads.
Do my M-Pesa wallet credits expire at the end of the month?
No. Your wallet balance rolls over indefinitely until you choose to unlock a matched lead. There are zero recurring monthly subscription charges.
Am I charged automatically when a new job is posted?
Never. You can preview the job category, title, neighborhood, urgency, scope, and stated client budget completely free. Your wallet is only deducted KES 200 when you explicitly click "Unlock Contact."
Do you take a percentage commission on the job value I win?
No. You keep 100% of the job value you negotiate and invoice to your client. BonnyRigg only charges the flat KES 200 lead unlock fee.
